Doctoral research

Photochromic Receptors for Nucleotide Detection Using Machine Learning Techniques

PhD in Chemistry · IISER Kolkata · 2026

Doctoral research investigated azobenzene-based photochromic molecular systems designed to interact with biologically relevant analytes. Rather than relying exclusively on individual spectroscopic responses, the work explored how patterns embedded in experimental data could be analysed using multivariate statistical and machine-learning techniques. Methods including Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) were used to investigate chemical-response patterns and analyte discrimination.

How can molecular and experimental chemical data be transformed into reliable, interpretable predictions?
